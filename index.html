About

I am a PhD candidate at the Department of Mathematics, University of Bari Aldo Moro. My research focuses on topological data analysis, optimization, computational geometry, and machine learning. In 2026 I was a visiting research student at the University of Southampton (Electronics and Computer Science Department), working with Dr Andersen Ang.

PhD project: Topological Data Analysis and Optimization for Industrial Processes (D.M.117/23 co-financed by Pirelli Tyre S.p.A.)

Research Experience

  • Visiting Research Student, University of Southampton, Electronics and Computer Science Department, UK (Feb 2026 – Jul 2026). Supervisor: Dr Andersen Ang. Developed T-ARC, a topology-aware clustering framework with convergence analysis (Best Poster Award, LOGML 2026).
  • Visiting Researcher, Pirelli Digital Solution Center, Bari, Italy (Mar 2025 – Aug 2025). Low-rank dimensionality reduction methods for the analysis of tyre materials.
  • Consultant Researcher, Pirelli Tyre S.p.A., Milan, Italy (Oct 2023 – present). Topological machine learning framework predicting tyre noise from tread pattern images.
